## 3. Prep tasks (do these before January 2027)

1. **Kenyan legal entity + compliance pack.** Company limited by shares registered on eCitizen/BRS; **KRA PIN**; Certificate of Incorporation, CR12, Tax Compliance Certificate; corporate bank account (many grants disburse only to a company account, not M-PESA). AECF/GSMA/develoPPP all require these plus audited or management accounts.
2. **Traction metrics dashboard.** Because you are pre-revenue, define and instrument proxy metrics now: registered farmers onboarded, alerts delivered (SMS/Swahili vs English split), Soko listings and claimed delivery runs (`GET /api/soko/stats` already counts these), riders active, arbitrage recommendations acted on, and — most persuasively — **measured KES uplift per farmer per sale vs. their default market.** Almost every funder above scores on evidence of impact per beneficiary.
3. **Pitch deck (10–12 slides) + 2-minute demo video.** One deck, three variants: grant/impact (GSMA, AYuTe, KCIC, FAO), investor (Catalyst, Antler, Katapult, Norrsken), and prize/narrative (Africa Prize, GoGettaz, ABH). Video should show the web dashboard and both Expo apps on a real phone. The `/pitch` page in Mission Control is a print-ready one-pager to start from.
4. **18–36 month financial model + unit economics.** Cost per farmer served, cost per delivery run, Gemini/API cost per recommendation, and path to revenue (commission on Soko, subscription, or per-delivery fee). Africa Prize and develoPPP explicitly coach or screen on financial modelling.
5. **Impact & M&E framework.** Baseline + target numbers on smallholder income, food loss avoided, CO₂e avoided via e-boda vs. petrol boda, and gender/youth disaggregation. Map to SDG 1/2/13. Needed verbatim by develoPPP, GSMA, MIT Solve, UNDP timbuktoo.
6. **Cap table, IP and founder docs.** Founder shareholding, any advisor equity, IDs/passports, CVs, and a clear statement that the codebase and models are owned by the company. Antler, Catalyst, Norrsken and Katapult are all equity investors and will diligence this.
7. **Two or three signed partnership/LOI letters.** A county agricultural office, a farmer cooperative or aggregator, and an e-boda fleet or rider SACCO. Letters of intent substitute effectively for revenue at pre-revenue stage in grant scoring.
8. **Standing profiles + alerts.** Create profiles on [VC4A](https://vc4a.com/), [TEFConnect](https://tefconnect.com/), and Seedstars/Startup Map Africa; subscribe to KCIC, UNDP Kenya and GSMA M4D mailing lists. Several 2026 windows above were 4–6 weeks long, so notification speed is the binding constraint.
